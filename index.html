Frequently Asked Questions

Are Skechers shoes machine washable?
Yes! Many Skechers styles feature a Machine Washable icon. Wash on cold gentle cycle and air dry for best results.
What makes Hands Free Slip-ins different?
Our molded Heel Pillow holds your ankle snugly without crushing the back heel counter when stepping in.
How do I choose between Memory Foam and Arch Fit?
Memory Foam offers soft instant cushioning for casual walking, while Arch Fit provides structured orthotic support for long standing hours.
